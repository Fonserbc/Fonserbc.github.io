Description

Off-Score is a small international team formed for the development of Off-Score: A game of songs aiming to make games around music, and to provide listeners with a playful experience that highlights the lyrics and enhances the stories of the songs.

Name

The name Off-Score comes from our vision to take people out of the "game score" and "music score", steer them "off-score".

Ferran Bertomeu Castells

Taiwan-based Catalan game designer & programmer. 10+ years professional experience in game programming, including Disney Spellstruck and Monkey-catching Game.

YenTing Locale

Award-winning Taiwanese jazz singer-songwriter, music producer and cross-media creator. Past music releases include 4 albums and 3 EPs. 10+ years experience working in interactive work with Ferran.

Vanja LoVerso

Freelance concept artist, illustrator and visual designer. Working in videogames, software and app development, including Testflight, Flightpath, Skyrocket and Disney Spellstruck.
