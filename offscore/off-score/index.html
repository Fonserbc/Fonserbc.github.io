Description

“Off-Score: A game of songs” is a surreal music puzzle game that transports you into the stories of our original fusion-jazz songs. Through a mysterious vinyl player sitting on a songwriter's desk, explore the magical worlds that unfold along with the music and lyrics.

From a piece of paper striving to survive in the society of a copy machine, to an elephant hunter that struggles with regrettable memories, through a song-sized universe that unfolds as you change your point of view. These songs will bring you to different worlds of fantasy and secrets.

Features

  • 3 interactive songs, each with its unique story and mood
  • Point, click & drag puzzle gameplay
  • Original jazz-singer-songwriter songs written by YenTing Lo
  • Listening is a big part of the play experience
  • Each song's gameplay is strongly connected to its story

About Off-Score Team

Off-Score is a small international team formed for the development of Off-Score: A game of songs aiming to make games around music, and to provide listeners with a playful experience that highlights the lyrics and enhances the stories of the songs.
